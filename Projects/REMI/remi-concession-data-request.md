## Email

**To:** Minerals Commission, Mining Cadastre / GIS unit \[address\] **Cc:** Tarkwa regional office \[address\] **Subject:** Request for mineral rights boundary data (Prestea–Tarkwa area) for a research project

Dear Sir/Madam,

I am \[name\], \[role, e.g. a student / researcher at …\]. I am building **REMI**, a research tool that uses free satellite imagery (Sentinel-2 and Landsat) to detect land change that may indicate illegal small-scale mining or deforestation, so that it can be passed to the relevant authorities for verification on the ground.

To avoid flagging **licensed** operations as suspected illegal activity, the tool needs to know where mineral rights have been granted. I would therefore like to request the boundaries of mineral rights within the following area of the Western Region:

- **Area:** longitude 2.20° W to 1.90° W, latitude 5.30° N to 5.60° N (WGS 84), covering Prestea, Bogoso and Tarkwa, in the Prestea-Huni Valley and Tarkwa-Nsuaem municipalities.
- **Licence types:** large-scale mining leases, small-scale mining licences, prospecting and reconnaissance licences, and any areas reserved or closed to mining.
- **For each licence, if available:** licence type, holder, mineral, date granted, expiry date, current status (active, suspended, revoked, expired), and area.
- **Format:** a GIS file (shapefile, GeoPackage or KML) is ideal. A table of corner coordinates, or an extract from the Mining Cadastre Administration System, would also be very helpful.

If the data are available only for a fee or under a data-sharing agreement, I would be glad to know the procedure and the cost. The data would be used only for this research, not redistributed, and every alert the tool produces is described as **"suspected"** and checked by a person before any action is considered. I would be happy to share the results with the Commission.

Thank you for your time. I look forward to hearing from you.

Yours faithfully, \[Name\] \[Affiliation\] \[Phone\] · \[Email\]

If they don't reply

- **After 2 weeks:** follow up by phone, and send the email again to the regional office.
- **Ask in person** at the Tarkwa office if you're in the area. Cadastre extracts are often printed or exported on the spot.
- **Fallbacks:** the public cadastre portal reported in 2016 at `ghana.revenuesystems.org` (check whether it still loads and shows boundaries); GHEITI reports list licences but without boundaries; research groups (e.g. Clark University's 2016 Ghana mining study) may share their concession layers.
- **Until then**, Layer 6 reports every alert's licence status as **"unknown"**.
