# Reading Session

## Bible:  1st John Chapter 1 vs 2


John said to the men whom were sent to know who he was, "In the begining was the word, the word was with God, and the word was God. He was withh God in the begining, through him all things were made, and without him nothing has been made that was made. In him was life and that life was the light of all mankind. The light shines in the darkness and the darkness has not overcome it. There was a man who was sent from God, as a witness to testify concerning the light, so through him all might believe. He himself was not the light,, he came as a witness to the light.

The true light that gives light on  to everyone was coming, and though the world was made through him, the world didnt recognize him, Yet to those who believes in his name and recieves him, he gives them the right to become the child of God. Children born not of natural descent, or by a husbands will, but by the will of God." This was the confession John the baptist gave to the men sent to know who he was, and they asked him questions. John through his confessions specified he wasnt the Messiah, then they asked him, "Are you Elijah?", of which John the baptist replied no. Then they asked him whom he was. of which he replied, "I am the one calling from the wilderness, Telling you make straight the path for the LORD". So the Pharasees then asked him, if you are not a prophet then why do you baptize.

John replied, I only baptize with water.  But there exists one amongst you of which you do not know. This happened on the side of Jordan at Bethany, where John the baptist baptizes.
