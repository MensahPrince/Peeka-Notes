# JESUS TURNS WATER INTO WINE.

And on the third day there was a wedding. Jesus' mother had been invited, as well as Jesus and his disciples. They run short of wine, and Jesus' mother went to Jesus. "Woman, why do you involve me?" , Jesus replied. "My time has not yet come". His mother revealed to the servants to do as Jesus told them. And so he directed them to fill six jars with water to the brim. And when they had done that, He instructed them to  draw some out and take it to the master of the banquet.  And they did so. And so the master had tasted the water that had been turned into wine. He did not know where the wine was from. Even though the person who drew it knew. The master of the banquet went to the bridegroom and told him, "Everyone brings the choice wine first and after every one has had too much to drink they then bring out the cheapest one, Yet you have saved  the best till now"  what jesus did there in cana of galilee was one of the first signs through which he revvealed his glory. And his desciples believed in him.


After this he went to Capernaum with his mother and his brothers and his disciples, There they stayed for a few days.


My observation after this is that when the servants were tasked to fill the six stone jars with water, the bible never said or indicated when exactly it turned to wine. how ever, it had already been turned to wine before the master of the banquet had taken a sip. Id like to use this to illustrate that we do not know when miracles are bound to happen, they just do. when the time and timing is just right. in this case it was between the moment after it was filled to the brim and before the master of the banquet had taken a sip. And it is often instigated by a greater someone. either by me in faith, or by God, the son and holy spirit. But actually, it I find that Jesus' mother had show the greatest faith, when she directed the servants to do what Jesus said. Knowing his directives would manifest into something great.


It shows,

- Trust
- Faith
