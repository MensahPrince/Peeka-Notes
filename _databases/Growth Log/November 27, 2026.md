# Jesus Teaches Nicodemus

---

There was a Pharasee, he was named Nicodemus, He was a member of the ruling council. The went to Jesus and said, Teacher, We know you are  a teacher whom has come from God because the signs you are doing was that of God . For no one could do those if God was not with him. Then Jesus replied, "Very truly I tell you. no one can see the kingdom of God unless they are born again." But you see Nicodemus asked Jesus, How can he be born again when he was old. How can a grown man be born again when He cant enter a second time to be born,

Then Jesus answered, " Very truly I tell you, No one can enter the kingdom of Heaven unless they are born of the water and the Spirit. Flesh gives birth to Flesh, Spirit gives birth to Spirit, You should not be surprised when I tell you this. You must be born again.

Nicodemus answered and said unto him, How can these things be? Jesus answered and said unto him, Art thou a master of Israel, and knowest not these things? Verily, verily, I say unto thee, We speak that we do know, and testify that we have seen; and ye receive not our witness. If I have told you earthly things, and ye believe not, how shall ye believe, if I tell you of heavenly things? And no man hath ascended up to heaven, but he that came down from heaven, even the Son of man which is in heaven. And as Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, even so must the Son of man be lifted up: That whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have eternal life. For God did not send his son to condemn the world, but to save the world through him.
